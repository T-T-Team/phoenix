# Phoenix Slot Machine
This mod adds vintage slot machine called Phoenix where you can play for various prices.

Simply place the block, insert valuable metals or gems, and you can play as long as your balance is above zero.

![slot machine UI](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/1829/275/slotmachine-png.png)

### What to insert?
You can by default insert Copper ingot, Iron ingot, Gold Ingot, Emerald, Diamond or Netherite ingot. Each material gives different value.

### How to play?
After inserting materials your balance is displayed in bottom right corner. If it is not zero, you can play on lower game.

### Winning combinations
Around the Phoenix GUI you can see several bars with winning combinations you can get. In lower play you can only win with middle line. In advanced play winning combinations are different, more rewarding and there are 5 winning lines.

### Advanced play
To play advanced play you need to win something in lower play first. After reaching enough credits in "Multiwin" balance you can switch between playing on lower or advanced play simply pressing "Advanced play" button.

### Multiwin
Multiwin is separate balance bar (in middle) from your main deposit balance. This balance is used for playing on advanced play and for payout section.

### Bet
The more you bet the more you win. If you get bored for playing for only 1 credit you can multiply your bet by pressing "Bet" button. This also multiplies you winnings.

### Hold
Hold buttons are only used for lower play. After you spin and do not get winning combination you can hold up to two cylinders for your next spin. But you can not use hold function after winning spin or two times in row. 

### Risk
After getting winning combination your winning credits are transferred into "Risk" deposit right above your main balance and the "Risk" minigame is triggered. Now you can double your current winnings by trying to match flashing pictures of Hearts and Clubs by pressing corresponding button. If button you press is matching the flashing picture your winnings get doubled, and you can continue or simply press "Start" button to end Risk minigame and your winnings will be transferred to "Multiwin" balance. But beware, if you miss match symbols you will lose all winnings.   

### Transfer Multiwin
If you are running low on you main deposit, and you still have some credits in Multiwin, you can transfer some credits from there to your main deposit by clicking on "Transfer Multiwin" button in upper right corner. But be careful, the amount of transferred credits is based on your current bet.

### Payout
Payout button (in upper right corner) takes you to the Payout section where you can withdraw items for your Multiwin credits. 

### Joker
Joker is the "wild" symbol that can substitute every symbol in the game giving you higher chance to win. By default, joker can occur only in lower play.
